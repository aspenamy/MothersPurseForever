# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release for World of Warcraft: Forever, made from Mother's Purse 0.1.0 for retail.

- Hover an item in your bags and press Space to mark it as junk or a favorite, or Shift+Space to mark it for the auction house.
- Junk is greyed out with a torn slash, favorites get a blue bookmark and auction items a gold coin that glows while the auction house is open.
- The tooltip says what the next press will do.
- Grey items count as junk unless you mark them as something else, and bound items can't be marked for auction.
- Blizzard's Sell All Junk button sells your junk marks along with the grey items, 12 per click, and never sells favorites or auction items.
- Marks in the bank and Warband bank, across all 9 of Forever's tabs for each.
- Works with Baganator, Bagnon and Auctionator, and turns off its support for any of them that stops lining up after an update.
- Settings page under Options > AddOns > Mother's Purse Forever, and `/purse` commands.
